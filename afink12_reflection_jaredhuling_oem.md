The gap in this project was very easy to find, as most of the timeline shows inactivity. There is a large spike at the start, with the commits coming from one author. This indicates that this author finished the project very quickly. However, there aren't any commits indicating the finalization of the project until a few years later, when a new author continuously adds commits to the project. This is likely because the original author was waiting for the secondary author to provide support and guidance to finalize the release.
